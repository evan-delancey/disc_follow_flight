DiscTrail

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

DiscTrail does not collect, store, or share your personal information. All video processing happens entirely on your device.

1. Who We Are

DiscTrail ("the app") is developed and maintained by Evan DeLancey. If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach us at:

privacy@disctrail.app

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Videos or images you load into the app
  • Location data or GPS coordinates
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash reports

3. On-Device Processing

All disc tracking and flight path analysis is performed locally on your device. Videos you select are read directly from your photo library or file system and are never uploaded to any server. No video data leaves your device.

4. Device Permissions

The app may request the following permissions:

  • Photo / Media Library — to let you select a disc golf video for analysis. We do not access any other files or photos.
  • Storage (Android) — to save exported flight-path images to your device. Files are written only where you direct them.

You can revoke these permissions at any time through your device settings. Revoking them will limit the app's functionality.

5. Subscriptions & Payments

DiscTrail offers an optional paid subscription processed entirely by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment information. Billing, receipts, and subscription management are handled directly by Google under their own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

To cancel your subscription, visit Google Play → Subscriptions on your device.

6. Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The only third-party service used is Google Play Billing for subscription management, as described above.

7. Children's Privacy

DiscTrail is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Because the app collects no personal data at all, it is safe for users of all ages.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and post the revised policy here. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

9. Contact

Questions or concerns? Email us at privacy@disctrail.app.
